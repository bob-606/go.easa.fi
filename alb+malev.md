## Overview

This document provides a detailed cost estimate for a split training pathway:

- **Stage 1 (SPL with TMG privileges):** Completed at **ALB Aviation (Romania)** – a DTO that explicitly offers SPL(TMG) training with a TMG aircraft (SF25 Falke).
- **Stage 2 (LAPL(A)) and Stage 3 (PPL(A)):** Completed at **MALÉV AERO CLUB ATO (Hungary)** – an ATO with significantly cheaper hourly rates for aeroplane training.

The student has already completed the theoretical knowledge for the SPL; no further theory costs are incurred.

## Key Assumptions

1. **Theory already completed** – no theory course or exam costs are included.
2. **Skill test / examiner fee** – estimated at €150 per test (based on MALÉV’s published fee).
3. **Licence issue fees** – estimated at €100 per licence (varies by NAA; actual may differ).
4. **TMG rental rate at MALÉV** – assumed to be €110/hr (based on your quote). If the actual rental rate differs, adjust Stage 2 accordingly.
5. **21 hours post‑endorsement** – all flown solo or as PIC on TMG; no instructor needed.
6. **PPL(A) training** – uses the Cessna 150 at MALÉV’s package rate of €163/hr (derived from their PPL package of €7,335 for 45 hours). If you pay as you go, confirm the exact hourly rate.

## Recommended Next Steps

1. **Contact MALÉV AERO CLUB** to confirm:
   - That they have a TMG aircraft available (e.g., SF25 Falke or similar).
   - The **exact TMG rental rate** (solo) – is it €110/hr or different?
   - Whether they are familiar with the SPL(TMG) → LAPL(A) conversion pathway and will accept your SPL from ALB.

2. **Contact ALB Aviation** to confirm their SPL‑TMG course dates and availability.

3. **Get written quotations** from both schools based on your specific path.

4. **Budget for extra hours** – most students require more than the regulatory minimums.
