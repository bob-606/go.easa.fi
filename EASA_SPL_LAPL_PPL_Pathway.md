# EASA Licensing Pathway: SPL(TMG) → LAPL(A) → PPL(A)

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** August 2026  
**Regulatory Basis:** Part-FCL (Reg. (EU) No 1178/2011) and Part-SFCL (Reg. (EU) 2018/1976)

---

## Core Legal Framework

| Licence | Regulatory Framework | Governing Regulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SPL** (Sailplane Pilot Licence) | Part-SFCL | Annex III to Reg. (EU) 2018/1976 |
| **LAPL(A)** / **PPL(A)** (Aeroplane Licences) | Part-FCL | Annex I to Reg. (EU) No 1178/2011 |

---

## TMG Classification Under EASA

A **Touring Motor Glider (TMG)** has a dual legal nature under EASA rules.

| Framework | Classification | Licence Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Part-SFCL** | TMG is a **privilege** endorsed on an SPL | SPL |
| **Part-FCL** | TMG is a **class rating** endorsed on an aeroplane licence | LAPL(A) / PPL(A) |

**Regulatory Definition (FCL.010):**
> *"‘Touring motor glider (TMG)’ means a specific class of powered sailplanes that has an integrally mounted, non-retractable engine and a non-retractable propeller. It shall be capable of taking off and climbing under its engine power according to its flight manual."*

**Key Point:** A TMG is legally a **powered sailplane**, not an aeroplane. This classification is the reason for the specific credit and conversion pathways described below.

---

## Stage 1: Zero → SPL (TMG Only)

**Goal:** Obtain a Sailplane Pilot Licence with privileges to fly TMGs only (no time in pure sailplanes).

| Requirement | Detail | Regulatory Reference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Minimum Age** | 16 years | FCL.010 |
| **Medical Certificate** | Valid EASA Class 2 or LAPL medical | — |
| **Theory Training** | Complete SPL theory course at an ATO or DTO | SFCL.135 |
| **Theory Exam** | Pass the SPL theoretical knowledge examination | SFCL.135 |
| **Flight Instruction** | **15 hours** on TMGs | SFCL.130(a)(2)(v) |
| **Dual Instruction** | At least 10 hours | SFCL.130(a)(2)(v) |
| **Solo Flight** | At least 2 hours | SFCL.130(a)(2)(v) |
| **Launches / Landings** | At least 45 as pilot-in-command (PIC) | SFCL.130(a)(2)(v) |
| **Solo Cross-Country** | At least 50 km (27 NM) | SFCL.130(a)(2)(v) |
| **Skill Test** | Conducted **on a TMG** | SFCL.145(b) |
| **Flight Instructor Required** | **FI(S)** (Flight Instructor Sailplane) | — |

**Outcome:** SPL with TMG privileges.

---

## Stage 2: SPL (TMG) → LAPL(A)

**Goal:** Convert the SPL(TMG) into a Light Aircraft Pilot Licence for aeroplanes (LAPL(A)) with an SEP class rating.

| Requirement | Detail | Regulatory Reference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Prerequisite TMG Experience** | 21 hours on TMGs **after** the TMG endorsement was added to the SPL | FCL.110.A(b) |
| **Theoretical Knowledge Assessment** | **NO separate written exam.** Oral assessment by the examiner during the skill test. | Appendix 1, point 1.4 |
| **Oral Exam Subjects** | (1) Operational procedures; (2) Flight performance and planning; (3) Aircraft general knowledge | FCL.135.A(a)(2) |
| **Aeroplane Training** | 3 hours of flight instruction in an SEP aeroplane | FCL.135.A(a)(1) |
| **Dual Take-offs / Landings** | 10 | FCL.135.A(a)(1)(i) |
| **Supervised Solo T/O & Landings** | 10 | FCL.135.A(a)(1)(ii) |
| **Skill Test** | **One** skill test in the SEP aeroplane (practical + oral theory combined) | FCL.135.A(a)(2) |
| **Experience Credit (TMG → SEP)** | Up to 50% of TMG time credited toward LAPL(A) hours (decided by DTO/ATO via pre-entry flight assessment) | FCL.110.A(c) |
| **Credit Limitation** | TMG credit **cannot** be used for supervised solo requirements | — |
| **Flight Instructor Required** | **FI(A)** (Flight Instructor Aeroplane) | — |

**Outcome:** LAPL(A) with SEP (single-engine piston) class rating.

---

## Stage 3: LAPL(A) → PPL(A)

**Goal:** Upgrade the LAPL(A) to a full Private Pilot Licence (PPL(A)).

| Requirement | Detail | Regulatory Reference |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Theoretical Knowledge** | **Full credit.** No additional written theory exams required. | Appendix 1, point 1.3 |
| **Additional Flight Time** | 15 hours on aeroplanes **after** the LAPL(A) is issued | FCL.210.A(b) |
| **Dual Instruction** | At least 10 hours in a training course at a DTO or ATO | FCL.210.A(b) |
| **Supervised Solo Requirement** | 10 hours supervised solo (including at least 5 hours cross-country) | Regulation (EU) 2024/2076 |
| **Instructor Qualification for Solo** | Must be conducted under a **PPL-qualified instructor** | Regulation (EU) 2024/2076 |
| **Critical Change (2024 Rule)** | LAPL(A) solo hours logged under a LAPL-only instructor **do NOT count** toward the PPL(A) solo requirement. You must redo the supervised solo hours under a PPL-qualified FI(A). | Regulation (EU) 2024/2076 |
| **Skill Test** | **One** PPL(A) skill test (practical only) | FCL.210.A |
| **Flight Instructor Required** | **FI(A)** qualified to instruct for PPL(A) | Regulation (EU) 2024/2076 |

**Outcome:** PPL(A).

## Total Flight Time Summary

| Stage | Hours | Aircraft Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Stage 1: 0 → SPL(TMG) | 15 hours | TMG |
| Stage 2: Prerequisite (post-endorsement) | 21 hours | TMG |
| Stage 2: Aeroplane Training for LAPL(A) | 3 hours | SEP |
